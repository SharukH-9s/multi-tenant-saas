# Code of conduct

Marlowe Works expects respectful behaviour in every channel: meetings, chat, email and customer calls.

## Conflicts of interest

Employees must disclose any financial interest in a supplier, customer or competitor. Gifts worth more than 50 euros must be declined or handed to the compliance officer.

## Speaking up

Concerns can be raised with a manager, with People Operations or anonymously through the ethics line. Retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith is a serious violation and leads to disciplinary action.
