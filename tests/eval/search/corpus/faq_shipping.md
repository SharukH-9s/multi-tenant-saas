# Shipping FAQ

## How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery within the country takes three to five working days. Express delivery arrives the next working day for orders placed before 14:00.

## Do you ship abroad?

We ship to all member states of the European Union. Customs duties for other countries are paid by the recipient.

## What if my parcel is damaged?

Photograph the damaged parcel and contact support within seven days. We send a replacement free of charge.
