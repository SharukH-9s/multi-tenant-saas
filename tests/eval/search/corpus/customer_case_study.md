# Case study: Brunner Metalworks

Brunner Metalworks, a family-owned workshop with 40 employees, adopted Marlowe to replace spreadsheets for order tracking.

## Results

After six months the workshop reduced late deliveries by 35 percent and cut the time needed to quote a job from two days to three hours. The inventory module removed the weekly manual stock count.

## Lessons learned

The team recommends starting with the Orders module only and adding Inventory after the first month.
