# Account FAQ

## I forgot my password

Choose Forgot password on the sign-in page. A reset link is sent to your email address and stays valid for 30 minutes.

## How do I add a teammate?

Owners and admins can invite teammates from Settings under Members. The invitation expires after seven days.

## How do I close my account?

Only an owner can close the workspace. All data is deleted 30 days after closure, and until then the closure can be undone.
