# Data retention policy, version 1

Effective 1 January 2022. Superseded by version 2.

Business records, including contracts, invoices and personnel files, are kept for five years after the end of the financial year in which they were created. After that period they are deleted by the records team.

Customer support tickets are kept for two years. System logs are kept for ninety days.

Exceptions to the retention periods require written approval from the compliance officer.
