# Product specification: SKU-4471 pressure valve

SKU-4471 is a stainless steel pressure relief valve for industrial water systems.

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Nominal diameter | 25 mm |
| Maximum working pressure | 16 bar |
| Operating temperature | -20 to 120 degrees Celsius |
| Connection | G1 threaded |
| Warranty | 24 months |

The valve ships with a calibration certificate. Replace the sealing ring every 36 months or after 10,000 pressure cycles, whichever comes first.
