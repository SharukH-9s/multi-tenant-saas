# Onboarding guide

## Before day one

People Operations ships the equipment in advance. New hires receive a laptop on day one, together with a security key and a welcome pack.

## First week

Each new hire is paired with an onboarding buddy who answers day-to-day questions. The first week includes a tour of the product, a security briefing and a meeting with the team lead to agree on goals for the first ninety days.

## Probation

The probation period lasts six months. A review takes place after three months and again at the end of probation.
