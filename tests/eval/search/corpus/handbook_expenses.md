# Expense reports

Submit every expense report within 30 days of the purchase date. Reports submitted later are paid only with a director approval.

## Receipts

A receipt is required for every expense above 25 euros. For smaller amounts a short note describing the purpose is enough.

## Travel

Book flights and trains through the company travel desk. Hotel stays are reimbursed up to 140 euros per night in cities and 110 euros elsewhere. Meals during travel are reimbursed up to 45 euros per day.

## Reimbursement timing

Approved reports are paid with the next monthly payroll run, which happens on the last Friday of each month.
