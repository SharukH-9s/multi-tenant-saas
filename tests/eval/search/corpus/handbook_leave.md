# Leave policy

## Annual leave

Every full-time employee at Marlowe Works receives 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues monthly, so a new hire who starts in July has roughly half of the entitlement available in their first year.

Unused vacation days roll over into the next year. An employee carries over up to ten days, and anything above that limit expires on 31 March unless a director approves an extension in writing.

## Sick leave

Sick leave is separate from annual leave. Staff may take up to fifteen paid sick days per year without a doctor note for absences of two days or fewer. Longer absences need a medical certificate.

## Parental leave

New parents are entitled to sixteen weeks of fully paid parental leave, which may be taken in one block or split into two blocks within the first twelve months.
