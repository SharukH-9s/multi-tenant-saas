# Incident response runbook

## Severity levels

Severity 1 means the product is down for most customers. Severity 2 means a major feature is degraded. Severity 3 covers minor issues with a workaround.

## First steps

The on-call engineer acknowledges the page within five minutes, opens an incident channel and names an incident commander.

## Targets

For severity 1 incidents the recovery time objective is 4 hours and the recovery point objective is 15 minutes.

## After the incident

A blameless post-incident review is held within five working days. Action items are tracked until they are closed.
