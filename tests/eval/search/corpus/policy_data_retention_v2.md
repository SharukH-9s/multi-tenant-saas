# Data retention policy, version 2

Effective 1 January 2025. This version replaces version 1.

Business records, including contracts, invoices and personnel files, are kept for seven years after the end of the financial year in which they were created. After that period they are deleted by the records team.

Customer support tickets are kept for three years. System logs are kept for one year.

Exceptions to the retention periods require written approval from the compliance officer and the legal department.
