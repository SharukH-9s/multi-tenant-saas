# Remote and hybrid work

Marlowe Works supports hybrid work. Employees may work from home up to three days per week, agreed with their team lead.

## Core hours

Everyone must be reachable between 10:00 and 15:00 local time. Meetings outside the core hours need the consent of all attendees.

## Home office stipend

A one-time home office stipend of 600 euros is paid after the probation period. It covers a desk, a chair or a monitor. Receipts must be submitted within sixty days of purchase.

## Working from abroad

Working from another country for more than twenty days in a year needs approval from People Operations because of tax and insurance rules.
