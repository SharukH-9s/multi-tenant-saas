# Site operations guide

This guide describes the operating routine of every Marlowe production site. Each section lists the weekly maintenance window, the duty manager and the safety conventions of the site.


## Site Lindholm

The Lindholm site performs its weekly maintenance every Tuesday at 03:00 local time. The duty manager at Lindholm is Karin Aho, who coordinates a crew of 12 technicians. Safety vests at Lindholm are blue, and visitors must sign in at the gatehouse before entering the hall. During the maintenance window at Lindholm the conveyor lines are stopped and the compressed air supply is isolated. Any deviation from this routine at Lindholm must be recorded in the shift log and reported to the regional operations office by the end of the shift.
