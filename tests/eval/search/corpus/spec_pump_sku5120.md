# Product specification: SKU-5120 circulation pump

SKU-5120 is a variable-speed circulation pump for heating loops.

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Flow rate | up to 4.2 cubic metres per hour |
| Power consumption | 8 to 45 watts |
| Maximum system pressure | 10 bar |
| Noise level | below 38 dB(A) |
| Warranty | 36 months |

The pump adjusts its speed automatically to the heating demand. A red indicator light means the rotor is blocked and the pump must be vented.
