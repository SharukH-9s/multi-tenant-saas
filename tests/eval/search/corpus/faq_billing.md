# Billing FAQ

## When am I invoiced?

Invoices are issued on the first day of each month. Payment is due within 30 days.

## Can I get a refund?

Annual plans can be refunded in full within 14 days of purchase. After that, unused months are credited to your account, not paid out.

## How do I change my payment method?

Open Settings, choose Billing and select Update payment method. The change applies from the next invoice.

## Do you charge sales tax?

We add VAT or sales tax where the law requires it, based on the billing address.
