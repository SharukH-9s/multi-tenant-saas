# Compliance handbook

This handbook collects the operating rules that apply across Marlowe Works. Each rule is described in its own section.


## Rule: supplier audits

Supplier audits are scheduled by the quality team. Responsibilities, records and reporting lines for supplier audits are described in the internal process documents, and the compliance office reviews them every year together with the internal audit team. Questions about how the rule applies to a specific case should be raised with the compliance office, which keeps a log of all decisions and publishes anonymised examples twice a year so that teams can learn from each other. The log is available to auditors on request, and it is reviewed during every external certification cycle so that the evidence stays complete and consistent.

The audit interval is eighteen months for critical suppliers and thirty-six months for all others. This limit is checked by the compliance office during its quarterly review, and exceptions need a written justification that is stored together with the decision log entry for the same period, so that the reasoning can be reproduced later by someone who was not involved.
