# Pricing plans

## Starter

The Starter plan costs 49 euros per month and includes up to five users and one factory site.

## Business

The Business plan costs 199 euros per month. It includes up to 50 users, three sites and priority email support.

## Enterprise

The Enterprise plan has custom pricing. It adds single sign-on, a dedicated account manager and a 99.95 percent availability guarantee.

All plans include the REST API. Annual billing gives two months free.
