# Glossary of acronyms

**SLA**: service level agreement, the promised availability and response times.

**RPO**: recovery point objective, the maximum amount of data loss measured in time that is acceptable after an incident.

**RTO**: recovery time objective, the maximum time allowed to restore a service after an outage.

**MFA**: multi-factor authentication, a sign-in that needs two or more proofs of identity.

**PTO**: paid time off, a general term for leave days such as vacation.

**KPI**: key performance indicator, a measurable value that shows progress towards a goal.
